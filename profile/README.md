# Accessible Maths

We are developing an accessible digital workbook that allows mathematics,
calculations and graphics to be manipulated and shared by students who
are blind or have low vision, educators and their peers. The project
targets upper secondary level mathematics. The workbook is a single
workspace to read, write, edit and share maths and perform calculations,
with multiple views of the same content to support collaboration: speech
through a screen reader, braille on a refreshable display, and documents
exchanged with Word and BRF.

Accessible Maths is a three-year ARC Linkage Project (LP240100506,
2025–2028), applying human-centred co-design methods with partners across
the education and accessibility sectors in Australia.

Project website: [accessiblemaths.org](https://www.accessiblemaths.org)
